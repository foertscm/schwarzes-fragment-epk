  • Schwarzes Fragment
    What is Schwarzes Fragment?

    Schwarzes Fragment is the live-proven dark electro / EBM project of Martin – melodic, energetic, dancefloor-ready.

    Most recently in April 2026 as live support for Kirlian Camera (Club From Hell, Erfurt — full recording in the Live Videos section below) and in May 2026 at the Dark Affair Festival in Leipzig during Wave-Gotik-Treffen weekend – the invitation to return in 2027 was extended during the performance itself.

    Active since 2008: debut album Seelenkiller in the Top 10 of the German Electronic Webcharts (December 2008), 6th place worldwide at the Sonic Seducer "Battle of the Bands". Based between Munich and Leipzig.
