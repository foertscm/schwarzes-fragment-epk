  • Schwarzes Fragment
    What is Schwarzes Fragment?

    Schwarzes Fragment is the live-proven dark electro / EBM project of Martin – melodic, energetic, dancefloor-ready.

    Most recently in April 2026 as live support for Kirlian Camera (Club From Hell, Erfurt — full recording in the Live Videos section below) and in May 2026 at the Dark Affair Festival in Leipzig during Wave-Gotik-Treffen weekend – the invitation to return in 2027 was extended during the performance itself.

    Active since 2008: debut album Seelenkiller in the Top 10 of the German Electronic Webcharts (December 2008), 6th place worldwide at the Sonic Seducer "Battle of the Bands". Based between Munich and Leipzig.

    Talks & Festival Appearances

    Martin speaks at tech and music festivals about AI as a tool in modern music production — inspiration and ideation, not push-button results. Songwriting and final decisions stay 100% human. This parallel tech career — from AR, robotics and deepfakes to generative AI · Oracle JavaOne Rockstar, Intel Black Belt, Intel Software Innovator — put the band project on stand-by in 2014. 280+ talks worldwide since then; today music and tech run in parallel. Co-speaker for both sessions: Ivo Moring (production credits including Roland Kaiser, Sarah Connor, DJ Ötzi's "Ein Stern"). The talks double as a "making-of" for Digital Phantom.
